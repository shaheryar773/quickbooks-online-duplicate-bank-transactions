# QuickBooks Online Duplicate Bank Transactions: Troubleshooting Guide

Duplicate bank transactions in QuickBooks Online can create confusion during bookkeeping and bank reconciliation. Repeated entries may make account balances appear incorrect and cause finance teams to spend extra time reviewing records.

However, not every repeated-looking entry is a genuine duplicate in the accounting ledger. It is important to identify where the duplication occurred before removing anything.

This guide explains how to investigate the issue safely.

## 1. Identify Where the Duplicate Appears

Start by checking whether the repeated entry appears in the bank feed or in the account register.

Common causes include:

- Downloading the same bank statement more than once.
- Manually entering a payment that later appears in the bank feed.
- Reconnecting a bank account and importing overlapping transactions.
- Adding a downloaded transaction instead of matching it to an existing record.

Understanding the source helps determine the appropriate correction.

## 2. Compare Transaction Details

Review the date, amount, description, and reference details of each suspected duplicate.

Compare these details with the original bank statement. Two transactions with the same amount may represent separate legitimate payments, so do not assume they are duplicates based on amount alone.

Also determine whether the transaction is still pending in the bank feed or has already been added to the accounting records.

## 3. Understand Exclude vs. Delete

QuickBooks Online users should understand the difference between excluding a downloaded bank-feed entry and deleting a recorded transaction.

**Exclude:** Generally used when an unwanted downloaded bank-feed item should not be added to the books.

**Delete:** Removes an existing transaction from the accounting records and may affect balances or reconciliation.

Do not delete a transaction simply because it looks similar to another entry. Confirm which record is incorrect first.

## 4. Verify the Account Reconciliation

After correcting a confirmed duplicate, review the account register and reconciliation status.

Compare the remaining records with the original bank statement. Confirm that legitimate deposits and payments are still accounted for and that the reconciled balance is accurate.

## 6. Prevent Future Duplicate Entries

A consistent bookkeeping routine can reduce the risk of repeated transactions:

- Review bank-feed activity regularly.
- Avoid importing the same statement period multiple times.
- Match downloaded entries to existing transactions when appropriate.
- Check manual entries against bank-feed records.
- Reconcile accounts using original bank statements.

## Conclusion

QuickBooks Online duplicate transaction issues should be investigated before any records are removed. Identify the source, compare transaction details, and choose the appropriate action to maintain accurate accounting records.
